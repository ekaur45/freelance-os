# How to Use This File

* Before quoting a new project, scan for a similar past example and use it as a sanity check against `EstimationRules.md` tier ranges.
* After completing a project, add an entry here (and cross-link the full case study in `Knowledge/04_Projects/` and `Knowledge/09_Portfolio/`) while the actual effort and pricing accuracy are still fresh.
* Note where an estimate was significantly off (over or under) and why — this is the fastest way to calibrate future estimates.
