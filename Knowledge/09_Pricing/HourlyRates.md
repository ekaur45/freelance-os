# Rate Variation Factors

Rates may reasonably vary based on:

* **Domain complexity** — healthcare (HL7/FHIR) and AI integration work typically command a premium over generic CRUD work due to specialized expertise.
* **Marketplace** — Upwork/Fiverr platform fees may justify a different displayed rate than direct client work to net the same effective rate.
* **Client type** — startups vs. enterprises may have different budget realities; adjust engagement structure (not necessarily rate) accordingly.
* **Relationship length** — long-term retainer clients may get a modestly reduced rate in exchange for consistent, predictable hours.

# Rate Increases

* Review rates periodically (e.g., annually or after a portfolio milestone) rather than ad hoc.
* Existing long-term clients should get advance notice before a rate change takes effect.
* New rates apply to new proposals; honor already-agreed rates for active engagements.
