# Direct Client Acquisition

## Purpose

A system for finding and converting clients outside freelance marketplaces.

## Channels

- LinkedIn networking
- Personal website
- Referrals
- Cold outreach
- Technical communities
- Partnerships

## Strategy

Focus on solving business problems instead of selling development hours.

## Positioning

Lead with:
- Industry expertise
- Previous solutions
- Business outcomes
- Technical credibility

## Process

1. Identify ideal clients
2. Research pain points
3. Send personalized outreach
4. Start conversations
5. Qualify requirements
6. Prepare proposal
7. Close engagement
