# Upwork Intelligence

## Platform Focus
Upwork rewards relevance, specialized positioning, strong profiles, fast responses, and proposals that solve the client's problem.

## Profile Strategy
- Position around outcomes, not only technologies.
- Highlight healthcare SaaS, AI integrations, full-stack systems, APIs, and scalable platforms.
- Maintain specialized profiles for high-value niches.

## Proposal Rules
- Lead with client problem alignment.
- Mention relevant architecture and previous experience.
- Ask focused discovery questions.
- Avoid generic introductions.
