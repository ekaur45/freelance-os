# AI Integration Service

## Service Summary

I help businesses integrate Artificial Intelligence into existing applications and new products using modern Large Language Models (LLMs), AI APIs, automation workflows, and retrieval systems. My focus is on delivering practical AI solutions that improve productivity, automate business processes, enhance customer experiences, and create measurable business value.

Whether integrating ChatGPT into an existing SaaS platform, building AI-powered assistants, implementing Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), or developing intelligent workflow automation, I build secure, scalable, and production-ready AI solutions.
