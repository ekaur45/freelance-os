# Technical Consulting Service

## Service Summary

I provide technical guidance for businesses that need an experienced engineering perspective before committing to a build — architecture decisions, technology selection, code/system audits, and technical due diligence. This service is advisory: the output is clarity and a recommendation, not necessarily code.

---

# Service Overview

This service includes:

* Architecture Review & Recommendations
* Technology Stack Selection
* Technical Due Diligence (pre-investment or pre-acquisition)
* Code & System Audits
* Scalability & Performance Assessments
* Build vs. Buy Analysis
* Technical Roadmap Planning
* Second Opinion on Existing Technical Decisions

---

# Business Problems Solved

Clients typically hire me to:

* Validate an architecture or technology choice before building
* Get an independent audit of an existing codebase or system
* Assess technical risk before an investment or acquisition
* Decide between building custom software or using existing tools
* Plan a technical roadmap for scaling a product
* Get a second opinion when their internal team is divided on an approach

---

# Typical Deliverables

* Written assessment report with findings and recommendations
* Architecture diagrams (as needed)
* Prioritized list of risks and recommended next steps
* Technology comparison/selection rationale
* Follow-up discussion to walk through findings

---

# Areas of Expertise

* Backend architecture (.NET, Node.js, PHP, Python ecosystems)
* Database design and scalability

# Common Client Problems Solved

* Uncertainty about whether current architecture will scale
* Need an outside expert opinion before a major technical investment
* Inherited codebase with unknown risk before acquiring or extending it
* Internal team lacks the specific expertise needed for a decision (e.g., healthcare interoperability, AI integration)
* Too many technology options with no clear way to decide

# Discovery Questions

When appropriate, ask:

* What decision or question is this consultation meant to answer?
* Is there existing code/architecture to review, or is this a pre-build decision?
* Who is the audience for the findings (technical team, investors, leadership)?
* What's the timeline for the decision this consultation informs?
* Is a written report required, or is a discussion sufficient?

# Notes for AI

Consulting is a lower-volume, higher-trust service — typically appropriate for clients who explicitly ask for an opinion, audit, or review rather than a build. Keep proposals for this service focused on the specific decision the client needs to make, and be explicit that the deliverable is an assessment/recommendation rather than implementation, unless the client wants both.
