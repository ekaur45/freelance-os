# Professional Summary

I am a Full Stack Software Engineer focused on designing and developing scalable web applications, SaaS platforms, healthcare solutions, and enterprise software. My work spans the complete development lifecycle, from understanding business requirements and designing system architecture to implementation, deployment, optimization, and long-term maintenance.

My strongest expertise lies in backend development using .NET and Node.js technologies, complemented by modern frontend frameworks for building responsive, user-friendly interfaces. I have extensive experience designing RESTful APIs, integrating third-party services, implementing authentication and authorization systems, and building secure, maintainable software.

I particularly enjoy solving complex business problems where software architecture, performance, scalability, and maintainability are critical. Rather than focusing solely on delivering features, I aim to build systems that remain reliable and easy to extend as products grow.

Healthcare software development is one of my core areas of expertise. I have worked with healthcare interoperability standards such as HL7 and FHIR, enabling secure integration between healthcare systems while maintaining compliance with industry requirements.

I also help businesses integrate AI capabilities into their applications, automate business processes, and modernize existing systems. My approach emphasizes practical solutions that deliver measurable business value instead of adopting technology for its own sake.

When working with clients, I value clear communication, realistic expectations, transparent project planning, and long-term relationships built on trust and consistent delivery. I strive to become a technical partner who understands both business objectives and engineering challenges.

My goal is to build software that is scalable, secure, maintainable, and aligned with the client's long-term vision while continuously expanding my expertise in modern software architecture, artificial intelligence, and cloud technologies.
