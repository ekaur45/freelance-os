# Discovery Questions

## Purpose

Discovery questions exist to reduce risk, clarify scope, and improve estimation accuracy — not to fill space or perform diligence theatrically.

Ask only what genuinely improves understanding of the project. Avoid long generic checklists sent to every client regardless of context.

---

# When to Ask Discovery Questions

* When the job description is vague or incomplete
* Before providing a fixed-price estimate
* When multiple technical approaches are possible and the right one depends on business context
* When integrations or third-party dependencies are unclear
* Before committing to a timeline

Skip discovery questions when the requirements are already clear and detailed — asking unnecessary questions can signal inexperience rather than diligence.

---

# Business Context Questions

* What problem is this project meant to solve?
* Who are the end users of this application?
* What does success look like for this project?
* Is this a new product, or are you replacing/extending an existing system?
* What's driving the timeline — is there a hard deadline?

---

# Feature & Scope Questions

* What are the must-have features for the first release versus nice-to-haves?
* Are there existing designs, wireframes, or specifications?
* Are there similar products or competitors you're using as a reference?
* What does the ideal user workflow look like, step by step?

---

# Technical Questions

* Is there an existing codebase, or is this a new build?
* Do you have a preferred technology stack, or is that open?
* What third-party services or APIs need to be integrated?
* Are there specific performance, scalability, or security requirements?
* Where will this be hosted, and is infrastructure already set up?

---

# User & Access Questions

* How many user roles are there, and what can each role do?
* Is authentication required? Any specific method (email/password, SSO, social login)?
* Are there multiple tenants/organizations, or a single user base?

---

# Integration & Data Questions

* What systems does this need to connect to?
* Is there existing data that needs to be migrated?
* Are there compliance or data residency requirements (e.g., healthcare, finance)?

---

# Project & Budget Questions

* Is the budget fixed, or is there flexibility based on scope?
* Is this a one-time build, or do you expect ongoing work after launch?
* Who will be the primary point of contact during the project?
* How do you prefer to communicate — async updates, regular calls, or both?

---

# AI-Specific Discovery (when relevant)

* What business problem should AI solve here?
* Should the AI reference your own data/documents, or is general knowledge sufficient?
* Are there compliance or data privacy constraints on what can be sent to an AI provider?

Refer to `Knowledge/03_Services/ai-integration.md` for more.

---

# Healthcare-Specific Discovery (when relevant)

* Which EHR/EMR or healthcare systems need to be integrated?
* Is the requirement HL7v2, FHIR, or both?
* Are there compliance requirements beyond standard data security?

Refer to `Knowledge/03_Services/healthcare-software-development.md` for more.

---

# How Many Questions to Ask

Prioritize the 3-5 questions that most affect scope, cost, or technical approach. Sending 20 questions upfront overwhelms the client and delays response. Ask the highest-impact questions first; follow up with details once engaged.

---

# Guiding Principle

Every discovery question should either reduce delivery risk or demonstrate genuine understanding of the problem. If a question does neither, don't ask it.
