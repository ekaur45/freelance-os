# Proposal Generation Rules

## Objective

Generate proposals that are client-focused, specific, and based on verified experience.

## Rules

1. Start with the client's problem, not personal introduction.
2. Match skills to the job requirements.
3. Use relevant projects only.
4. Avoid unsupported claims.
5. Keep proposals concise and outcome-focused.

## Selection Logic

Job keywords should determine:

- Relevant skills
- Relevant services
- Relevant projects
- Proposal tone

## Quality Checklist

- Does the opening show understanding?
- Are technical recommendations relevant?
- Is the call to action clear?
