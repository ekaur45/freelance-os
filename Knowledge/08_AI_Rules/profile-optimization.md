# Profile Optimization Rules

## Objective

Optimize freelancer profiles for search visibility and conversion.

## Rules

1. Lead with client outcomes.
2. Include high-value keywords naturally.
3. Highlight niche expertise.
4. Use measurable achievements where available.
5. Keep positioning consistent across platforms.

## Profile Sections

- Title
- Overview
- Skills
- Portfolio
- Specialized profiles
- Social proof

## Optimization Checklist

- Clear target client
- Clear services
- Relevant technologies
- Strong differentiation
